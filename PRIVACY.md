# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Anubis has no server, no account, no analytics, and no ads. It makes no network requests at all: the model ships inside the extension and posts are scored on your computer.

## What it reads

On x.com and twitter.com it reads the text of posts that are on or near your screen, and the author's handle. It does not read your messages, passwords, cookies, or any other site.

## What leaves your browser

Nothing. The extension requests no host permissions, so it cannot contact any server.

## What stays in your browser

- Settings, the block list, and flag counts, in extension storage.
- A cache of scores, in IndexedDB, keyed by a hash of the post text. It holds numbers, not text.
- Labels you give with the AI / Human buttons, in extension storage. These include the post text and handle, and they leave your browser only if you click Export on the options page.

Remove all of it by uninstalling the extension.
